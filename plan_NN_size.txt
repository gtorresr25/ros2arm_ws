For a 5DOF arm, you usually do not need a large ACT model unless doing:

complex dexterous manipulation,
cluttered scene reasoning,
multi-camera visuomotor control,
or long-horizon task planning.

A much more suitable embedded ACT configuration would be closer to:

hidden_dim: 128 or 256
dim_feedforward: 512–1024
enc_layers: 2
dec_layers: 2–4
nheads: 4
num_queries: 10–30

And preferably:

ResNet18 instead of ResNet50,
or even a MobileNet/EfficientNet backbone.

A practical embedded pipeline often looks like:

Camera
  ↓
Lightweight CNN
  ↓
Small Transformer / GRU
  ↓
Joint Targets
